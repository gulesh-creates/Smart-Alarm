Android Production Architecture

This prototype models the exact UX flows for your future Android app. In production on Android, web browser limitations are replaced with native APIs:

Exact Alarms Android uses AlarmManager.setExactAndAllowWhileIdle() + USE_EXACT_ALARM permission so alarms fire when phone is locked or deep sleeping.
Background Timer Browsers throttle background tabs. Production Android will use a ForegroundService with a persistent notification channel to guarantee countdown accuracy.
Face-Down Sensor Uses SensorManager listening to TYPE_ROTATION_VECTOR with a low-power partial wake lock, avoiding accidental wakeups.
Step Counting Uses native hardware Sensor.TYPE_STEP_DETECTOR or Google Health Connect, preventing battery drain from manual accelerometer calculations.
